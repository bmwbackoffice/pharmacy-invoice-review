Correspondence ({{ total }})

Emails the system read as correspondence — not a bill, payment or statement, so nothing else in the app picks them up — plus any other email (invoice, statement…) where a person wrote a request that's NEEDS REPLY waiting on us; its documents are still processed as usual. Go through each one: Handled (dealt with), Follow up (someone needs to act — say what in the note), or Dismiss. Forward sends it on (from the AP mailbox) — the email stays where it is, with a "Forwarded to …" line, until you mark it. A rule can match the sender, the subject, the To/CC addresses, or any combination, and either dismisses or forwards matching emails automatically (rules: Setup › Rules › Correspondence rules; people to forward to: Forward contacts).
