What the numbers mean
  • Emails received: emails that reached the invoice mailbox that day (Gmail's receive time), plus documents uploaded by hand on their upload day, by how the system read them. Other = spam, unknown, unreadable, or not read yet. One email can hold several records.
  • Bills recorded: invoice records saved that day, not counting copies set aside as duplicates. For QB leaves out bills kept out of QuickBooks (excluded vendors, "don't enter"). Sales orders count on the day they're recorded as the bill.
  • Payments / credit memos: records saved that day (payment duplicates left out).
  • Sent to review: items the automatic runs put in the review queue that day. An item that's answered, re-read and flagged again counts again.
  • Entered in QuickBooks: invoices and credit memos someone marked entered that day; the $ is the invoices. Automatic marks (a QuickBooks upload matching a bill, automatic settling) aren't counted, as on the dashboard.
  • Duplicates set aside: invoice and payment copies set aside as duplicates, automatically or by a person. A copy set aside the moment it was saved counts on its save day.
